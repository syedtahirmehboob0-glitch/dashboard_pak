Google’s Pakistan Office and the Next Phase of Digital Skills

Google opened its first Pakistan office in August 2026 as digital transformation and AI skills become larger parts of the technology agenda.

What happened

Pakistan’s Press Information Department reported on August 18, 2026 that the Prime Minister met a Google delegation and inaugurated Google’s first office in Pakistan. The discussion included digital transformation and youth IT/AI training.

Why an office matters

A local office can support engagement with policymakers, businesses, educators and developers, but an office alone does not guarantee investment or jobs.

Skills in demand

AI-assisted development, cloud, cybersecurity, data and product skills increasingly sit alongside traditional programming.

Practical advice

Build real projects, portfolios and communication skills. Certificates can complement evidence of capability.

Press Information Department
