Why the dollar rate matters

A higher USD/PKR number means more rupees are needed to buy one US dollar. This can raise the rupee cost of imported fuel, machinery and other foreign goods. It can also increase the rupee value of dollar-denominated remittances and overseas freelance income.

Reference rate vs. the rate you receive

USD/PKR reference data is useful for tracking the rupee, but it should not be confused with a cash-market, bank or exchange-company customer quote. Transaction rates can include spreads, fees and different market conditions. Always check the applicable provider rate before exchanging money.
