Methodology & data sources

Methodology and editorial standards

Independent publication: PakDigitalDashboard is not a government website. It organizes public information so readers can understand dates, sources and changes in Pakistan data.

Data sources

The dashboard prioritizes primary institutional sources already used across the site, including the State Bank of Pakistan, Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Pakistan Stock Exchange, Ministry of Finance and other identified public institutions. Individual pages identify their source and data date.

Dates and updates

A reported value is tied to the date supplied by its source. Where a live feed is available, the interface may refresh after the page loads. If a live request fails, the last known dated value should remain visible rather than being replaced by an empty state.

How we present data

We distinguish reported figures from interpretation. Calculators use the formulas implemented on the site and are presented as tools rather than official government calculations. Readers should consult the underlying institution for authoritative releases.

Editorial policy

Articles aim to summarize source material accurately, provide links to the original source and explain why a number matters without presenting commentary as official fact. Published dates and updated dates are shown where available.

Corrections

If a value, source attribution or explanation is incorrect, please report it through the feedback page or contact page. Corrections are reviewed against the underlying source.

Limitations

Public datasets can be revised, delayed or released on different schedules. A dashboard display should therefore be read with its source and data date, not as a guarantee of real-time completeness.

How we use data

PakDigitalDashboard presents public economic and civic information from the institutions that publish it. Figures retain the source, observation period and definition supplied by the source. The dashboard does not create official statistics.

Update process

Indicators are refreshed when the underlying source is available. Where a live refresh fails, the page should retain the last known published value rather than display an invented or blank figure.

Source hierarchy

Primary institutional publications are preferred, including Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, State Bank of Pakistan, Ministry of Finance, Pakistan Stock Exchange and relevant regulatory bodies. Secondary reporting is used for clearly identified editorial coverage when appropriate.

Corrections

If you identify an incorrect value, broken source link or misleading description, contact us with the page URL and the source supporting the correction.
