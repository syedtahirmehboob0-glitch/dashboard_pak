How the Rs 57.566T domestic public debt is structured

The Pakistan Economic Survey 2025-26 reports the end-March 2026 domestic public-debt stock at about Rs 57.6 trillion. Its component breakdown is:

  • Permanent debt: Rs 43.857T — mainly Pakistan Investment Bonds, Government Ijara Sukuk and Prize Bonds.
  • Floating debt: Rs 9.583T — mainly Market Treasury Bills.
  • Unfunded debt: Rs 3.215T — including National Savings instruments and Prize Bonds.
  • Other: Rs 0.910T — including Naya Pakistan Certificates, SDR on-lending and foreign-currency domestic debt.

These components total approximately Rs 57.565T because of rounding, matching the reported Rs 57.6T domestic-debt stock.

Broader debt-and-liabilities profile

For a different purpose, SBP's end-December 2025 debt-and-liabilities profile reported a broader stock of Rs 95.464T. The detailed categories include government domestic and external debt, IMF obligations, PSE debt, private-sector external debt, external liabilities, commodity operations and intercompany liabilities.

  • Government domestic debt: Rs 55.363T
  • Government external debt: Rs 23.166T
  • IMF debt: Rs 2.845T
  • Private-sector external debt: Rs 5.520T
  • Public-sector enterprises external debt: Rs 2.200T
  • PSE domestic debt: Rs 2.068T
  • External liabilities: Rs 3.383T
  • Commodity operations: Rs 1.086T
  • Intercompany external debt: Rs 1.538T
Important: the Rs 95.464T broader measure is not a second way of adding up the Rs 83.285T public-debt headline. The measures use different definitions, dates and consolidation treatment. The listed SBP categories can also overlap for analytical purposes, so they should not be mechanically summed.

How to read Pakistan's debt figures

Debt figures are not interchangeable. Public debt, government debt and broader debt-and-liabilities measures can use different definitions, consolidation rules and reporting dates. PakMetric keeps those measures separate so a visitor can trace each number to its source rather than combining incompatible totals.
