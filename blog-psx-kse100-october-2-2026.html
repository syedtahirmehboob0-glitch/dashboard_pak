Pakistan Stock Exchange: What Happened to the KSE-100 on October 2, 2026

A dated look at the latest KSE-100 session and the market factors reported at the time.

The session

PSX data showed the KSE-100 around 168,806.75 in an October 2 snapshot, while reporting on the completed Friday session put the close at 168,155, down 481 points or 0.29%.

Market context

Reports pointed to subdued participation, elevated crude-oil prices and geopolitical uncertainty. Selling was reported across several sectors while some oil and gas names provided support.

Why oil matters

Pakistan imports much of its energy, so global oil prices can affect the import bill, inflation pressure and business costs.

Reader takeaway

An index is a benchmark, not a forecast. One trading session should be read alongside company fundamentals, earnings and longer periods of market data.
