Pakistan’s September 2026 Economic Update: The Numbers to Watch

The Ministry of Finance published its September economic update, debt review and FY2027 borrowing plan.

The update

The Ministry of Finance listed its Monthly Economic Update and Outlook for September 2026 on September 30, alongside the Annual Debt Review FY2026 and Annual Borrowing Plan FY2027.

Why it helps

A monthly report brings several indicators into one period, but readers should still check each original institution and release date because data are published on different schedules and can be revised.

Debt versus borrowing

A debt review describes an existing debt stock. A borrowing plan describes intended financing. They answer different questions.

Practical rule

Compare monthly with monthly, fiscal-year with fiscal-year, and stock measures with flow measures.

Ministry of Finance
