How Pakistan fuel prices are updated

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) publishes petroleum-price notifications and related pricing information. OGRA's current portal lists a petroleum-products notification effective October 3, 2026, and its fuel-pricing pages also publish daily pricing data.

Because fuel rates can change, always check the source date shown on this page before using a figure for budgeting or business decisions.

Why this page matters: petrol prices affect household transport costs, delivery businesses, logistics and operating expenses. PakMetric provides the number together with its source date instead of presenting an undated figure.

What the published fuel price represents

A notified petroleum price is different from the final amount a customer may see in every commercial setting. Rates, taxes, margins and later notifications can change the amount. PakMetric therefore displays the feed date and links directly to OGRA's published petroleum-price information.
