What remittances are

Worker remittances are money sent to Pakistan by overseas Pakistanis. They provide foreign currency to recipient households and contribute to Pakistan's external accounts.

Why PakDigitalDashboard tracks them

Remittances can affect household income, consumption and foreign-exchange availability. PakDigitalDashboard presents the figure alongside exports, imports, the current account and exchange rates so visitors can see the broader external picture.

Data note: the dashboard value is a reference figure and its period should be checked against the latest SBP release. The latest published observation is August 2026; monthly flows can vary substantially.

How remittance data should be used

Monthly remittances are a flow during a reporting period, not a stock of money held by overseas Pakistanis. Month-to-month movements can reflect seasonal and other factors, so comparisons should use consistent periods and the latest SBP release.
