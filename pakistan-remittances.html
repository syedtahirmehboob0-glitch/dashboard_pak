What remittances are

Worker remittances are money sent to Pakistan by overseas Pakistanis. They provide foreign currency to recipient households and contribute to Pakistan's external accounts.

Why PakMetric tracks them

Remittances can affect household income, consumption and foreign-exchange availability. PakMetric presents the figure alongside exports, imports, the current account and exchange rates so visitors can see the broader external picture.

Data note: the dashboard value is a reference figure and its period should be checked against the latest SBP release. Monthly flows can vary substantially.
