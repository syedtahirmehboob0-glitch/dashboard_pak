$15 Million Green-Finance Proposal Could Support 800 Women-Led Startups

A proposed climate-finance project would combine seed funding, incubation and digital monitoring. It is a proposal, not a completed program.

The proposal

Reporting on October 3 said Punjab’s Environment Protection and Climate Change Department proposed a $15 million Gender-Responsive Climate Finance and Entrepreneurship Hub.

What it proposes

The reported design includes support for more than 800 women-led startups, training for 3,200 women and a target of more than 12,000 green jobs.

The technology component

A digital monitoring dashboard is proposed to track gender-disaggregated data and verified emission reductions.

Important distinction

The project is described as proposed and being processed. Targets should not be presented as funding or jobs already delivered.
