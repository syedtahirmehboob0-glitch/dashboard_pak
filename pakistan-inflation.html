What it means

CPI inflation measures the change in the average price level of a basket of consumer goods and services compared with the same period a year earlier. A 5% annual rate means the measured basket is about 5% more expensive than a year earlier, although individual household spending can change by more or less.

Why it matters

Inflation affects household purchasing power, business costs, interest rates and the value of savings. PakMetric also tracks the Sensitive Price Indicator (SPI) for a shorter-term view of selected everyday prices.

Data note: always check the reporting period and the latest PBS release before using a figure for a financial or policy decision.
