How Pakistan fuel prices are updated

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) publishes petroleum-price notifications and related pricing information. OGRA's current portal lists a petroleum-products notification effective October 3, 2026, and its fuel-pricing pages also publish daily pricing data.

Because fuel rates can change, always check the source date shown on this page before using a figure for budgeting or business decisions.

Why this page matters: petrol prices affect household transport costs, delivery businesses, logistics and operating expenses. PakDigitalDashboard provides the number together with its source date instead of presenting an undated figure.

What the published fuel price represents

A notified petroleum price is different from the final amount a customer may see in every commercial setting. Rates, taxes, margins and later notifications can change the amount. PakDigitalDashboard therefore displays the feed date and links directly to OGRA's published petroleum-price information.

How notified fuel prices should be read

Petroleum prices are time-sensitive. This page links to OGRA's notified petroleum-price information and its fuel-pricing portal so readers can verify the applicable notification rather than relying on an undated number.

What the published rate represents

A notified rate is a published price under the applicable pricing framework. It should not automatically be treated as a universal retail experience in every setting. Later notifications can supersede earlier ones, which is why the notification date is essential.

Frequently asked questions

Why can the petrol price change between two visits?

Fuel pricing is time-sensitive and new notifications can replace earlier rates. Check the source date shown on the page.

Why is the OGRA link important?

OGRA is the source linked by this page for notified petroleum-price information. It is the appropriate place to verify the applicable notification.

Does the notified price explain every amount paid by a customer?

Not necessarily. Commercial pricing, timing and applicable components can affect the amount a customer encounters. Verify the relevant notification.
