Privacy Policy

Information collected

Normal web requests can provide technical information such as IP address, browser and device characteristics, pages requested and referral information. The public dashboard does not require an account.

Cookies

The site may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functions, analytics and advertising. Browser settings can block or delete cookies, although some features or personalized advertising may be affected.

Google AdSense and advertising choices

When advertising is enabled, Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize advertising according to applicable policies and visitor choices.

For Google advertising controls, visit Google Ads Settings. You can review or turn off personalized advertising there. You can also use your browser controls to delete or block cookies, although this may affect some advertising or site functionality.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google requires publishers serving ads to use a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. The publisher must configure and enable its consent message in the AdSense account. If the consent controls are not available, contact the publisher before relying on personalized advertising choices.

Analytics

If analytics is enabled, aggregate usage information may be used to understand traffic and improve the site.

Public data

The site presents public institutional information with source attribution and does not claim ownership of underlying datasets.

Advertising consent

Where consent controls are presented, visitors can use them to review or change their choices. Google’s Privacy & messaging settings explain the consent options available to publishers.

Changes

This policy may be updated when advertising, analytics or other functionality changes.
