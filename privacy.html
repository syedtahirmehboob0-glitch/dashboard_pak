EEA, UK and Switzerland consent

For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, advertising consent is handled through Google's consent-management tooling when it is configured and published for this site. Google requires publishers serving personalized ads in these regions to use a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. Visitors can choose the available consent options presented by the banner and can change their choices through the consent controls.

Google's requirements and certified-CMP list can change, so the site's implementation is maintained against Google's current publisher guidance. See Google's EEA, UK and Switzerland CMP requirements.
