What Pakistan's CPI inflation rate actually tells you

Inflation explainer · Reviewed 3 October 2026

When you see a headline such as “Pakistan inflation,” the first question should be: which inflation measure and which period? The national Consumer Price Index is a weighted statistical measure of changes in prices across a defined basket. It is not a promise that every household's personal expenses rose by exactly the same percentage.

The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics reported national CPI inflation of 10.26% year-on-year in September 2026 and 1.27% month-on-month. The same release explains that national CPI combines urban and rural measures using weights. That means a household's experience can differ depending on its spending pattern, location and the prices it actually pays.

Year-on-year and month-on-month rates answer different questions. YoY compares September 2026 with September 2025 and is useful for seeing the change over a full year. MoM compares September with August and can show shorter-term movement. Neither number alone describes every price in the economy.

PakDigitalDashboard therefore labels the month and measure on its Pakistan inflation page. The primary source is the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics monthly inflation report.
